## Featured work: NexaStore

**[nexastorebd.com](https://nexastorebd.com)** · a multi-tenant commerce platform for Bangladeshi businesses. One account gives a shop its online store, its counter POS, its books and an AI sales assistant. I designed and built it, from database to deployment.

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **SaaS platform** | Multi-tenant with strict tenant isolation, custom domains per shop, plan-based features, subscription billing and renewals, self sign-up with approval |
| **E-commerce** | Storefront with a theme customizer, products and variants, brands, campaigns and discounts, courier booking and tracking, online payments, SEO and product feeds |
| **POS** | Installable offline-first till (PWA), barcode scanning, shifts and Z-reports, returns, credit sales, warranty and IMEI tracking, thermal receipts, labels and courier stickers |
| **ERP and finance** | Double-entry ledger, P&amp;L and balance sheet, purchases and suppliers, inventory valuation and COGS, approvals, fiscal periods, a simple mode for shopkeepers |
| **AI automation** | One inbox for Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp, where an AI assistant answers customers, finds products, quotes delivery and takes orders, with a hand-off to staff; AI product search on the storefront |
| **Engineering** | NestJS, Prisma and PostgreSQL behind a Next.js App Router frontend; Redis and BullMQ for queues, an outbox for events, Docker and CI/CD for deployment |
